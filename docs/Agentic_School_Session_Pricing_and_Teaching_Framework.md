# Agentic School
## Session, Pricing and Teaching Framework

## Standard Product

**Agentic Session**

**Duration:** 2 weeks

**Price:** USD 100 per individual learner

**Typical learner commitment:** 6–10 hours

**Recommended standard:** approximately 8 hours

## What Is a Session?

An Agentic Session is not simply two weeks of videos.

It is:

> **A two-week, outcome-based AI learning sprint designed to give a learner one practical and demonstrable capability.**

Every Session must answer:

**What will the learner be able to do after these two weeks that they could not confidently do before?**

## Recommended Eight-Hour Structure

### Orientation & Baseline

30 minutes

### Interactive AI-Led Learning

2 hours

### Guided Practical Labs

2 hours

### Personalized AI Tutoring

1 hour

### Applied Project

1.5 hours

### Assessment and Reflection

1 hour

Total:

**Approximately 8 hours**

The learner can distribute these hours flexibly across two weeks.

## Technical Sessions

More technical Sessions may require approximately:

**10–12 learner hours**

Examples:

- Building AI Agents
- RAG Systems
- AI Application Engineering
- AI-Assisted Software Development

Most additional time should be lab and project work rather than additional lecturer hours.

## Lecturer Economics

If a high-quality lecturer charges:

**$100/hour**

Agentic School should use that lecturer for activities where their contribution has high leverage.

For example:

A lecturer may spend:

- 2 hours designing/reviewing curriculum
- 1 hour recording expert insight
- 1–2 hours delivering a masterclass
- Periodic time reviewing advanced projects

The AI teaching system can then support many cohorts using the expert-approved curriculum.

This prevents the lecturer cost from becoming $300–$500 for every small group of learners.

## Session Economics Example

30 learners × $100:

**$3,000 revenue**

The Session may then carry costs for:

- AI usage
- Payment processing
- Content development
- Platform infrastructure
- Customer support
- Human supervision
- Marketing
- FMG administration

Human expert access can generate additional premium revenue rather than being included without limit in the $100 product.

## Cohort Versus Session

A **Session** is the educational product.

A **Cohort** is a group of learners progressing through that Session during roughly the same period.

This distinction matters.

Example:

**Session:** Building AI Agents

**Cohort:** September 2026 Global Cohort

The same Session may be delivered to many cohorts.

Because teaching is AI-first, learners may eventually begin Sessions more frequently rather than waiting for a lecturer-led class to open.

## Future Direction

Agentic School may eventually support:

**Fixed Cohort Mode**

Learners start together.

and

**Rolling Session Mode**

A learner can begin on multiple available start dates.

AI instructors make rolling international enrollment much easier because teaching is not dependent on one lecturer's time zone.

Supporting line:

That should become one of Agentic School's signature commercial ideas.
