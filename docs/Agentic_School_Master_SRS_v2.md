# AGENTIC SCHOOL
## An AI School for AI and Agents

**Document:** Master Software Requirements Specification  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Operating Entity:** FMG  
**Product Brand:** Agentic School  
**Platform Model:** Global AI Learning + Enterprise Transformation Platform  
**Core Philosophy:** AI-first, human-supervised

## 1. Product Vision

Agentic School is an international school for artificial intelligence, AI agents, AI-assisted work and AI-native organizations.

The school shall use artificial intelligence not only as a subject being taught, but as part of the teaching infrastructure itself.

The standard Agentic School learning experience shall therefore be delivered primarily through specialized AI teaching agents, learning laboratories, structured content and practical projects.

Human experts shall:

- Design curricula
- Supervise learning agents
- Review quality
- Conduct selected masterclasses
- Provide premium mentorship
- Review advanced projects
- Handle exceptional questions
- Deliver enterprise workshops
- Validate high-level professional competency where appropriate

The guiding principle is:

**Agents teach. Humans supervise, inspire and validate.**

## 2. Positioning

Agentic School shall position itself as:

> **The school for the agentic economy.**

Primary proposition:

> **Learn AI. Build Agents. Transform Work.**

Supporting proposition:

> **An AI-first school where intelligent agents teach alongside world-class human experts.**

Agentic School shall not position itself merely as:

- An LMS
- A collection of recorded videos
- A generic AI course website
- A certification seller
- A chatbot-based school

Its differentiation shall come from:

**AI-native education + practical building + skills verification + enterprise transformation.**

## 14. Definition of an Agentic Session

An **Agentic Session** shall be the standard commercial learning unit.

Standard duration:

**2 weeks**

Standard learner commitment:

Approximately **6–10 hours** depending on topic.

The Session shall focus on one practical capability.

The core rule is:

> **Every Agentic Session must produce one demonstrable capability in two weeks.**

## 15. Standard Session Price

The initial global standard individual price shall be:

**USD 100 per Agentic Session**

The system shall allow FMG administrators to modify pricing later.

Equivalent regional payment options may be displayed where necessary, but the core international reference price shall remain USD.

## 16. Autonomous Learning Model

The standard $100 Agentic Session shall be primarily AI-delivered.

A learner shall interact with an **AI Teaching Team** rather than relying on continuous live instructor delivery.

Typical teaching agents may include:

### Instructor Agent

Explains concepts and guides the curriculum.

### Tutor Agent

Answers questions and provides personalized explanations.

### Lab Coach Agent

Guides practical exercises.

### Builder Agent

Assists learners building AI workflows and projects.

### Code Mentor Agent

Supports developers with implementation and debugging.

### Assessment Agent

Runs formative assessments and skill checks.

### Project Coach Agent

Guides final practical work.

Approximately **8 hours**.

**10–12 hours**
